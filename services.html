Our Services

At Octane, we offer a full-service capability spanning general supply, logistics, cargo transportation, real-time tracking and road transportation.

01

General Supply

We supply a wide range of building and construction materials, mining and industrial equipment, as well as general office consumables including hospital equipment. We also carry out civil works, engineering fabrication and the supply of PPE attire.

Why it matters: one accountable supplier for materials, equipment and PPE reduces the number of vendors your team needs to manage.

02

Logistics

From paperwork through to compliant insurance protocols, Octane Transport has the know-how to best manage your consignment and make sure that everything has been considered ahead of time.

Why it matters: proactive documentation and insurance compliance reduces risk and delay at every handover point.

03

Cargo Transportation

Offering cargo transportation services throughout Zambia and the SADC region. Our customers include wholesalers and growers in the produce industry, and engineering-sector clients. Octane Transport maintains a well-serviced fleet of trucks to service our clients.

Why it matters: a maintained fleet and regional route experience means fewer breakdowns and missed deadlines.

04

Real-Time Cargo Tracking

Our team ensures that you can keep track of your cargo with our real-time tracking system — get exact information on where your shipment is, and become aware of any delays as soon as they happen.

Why it matters: full shipment visibility helps your operations and procurement teams plan with confidence.

05

Road Transportation

Whether it is moving farm equipment or transporting supplies from one side of the country to the other, Octane Transport will ensure that your products arrive safely and on time.

Why it matters: flexible road transport capability for equipment and general supplies, nationwide.
