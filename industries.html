Industries we serve

We service clients across a range of sectors, including agribusiness, mining, engineering, food & beverage, plastics & packaging and general logistics.

Agribusiness

Road transportation and cargo services for farm equipment, produce and agricultural supplies, connecting wholesalers and growers with their markets on time.

Mining

Transport and supply support for the mining sector, including the movement of machinery, equipment, spare parts and general supplies. Contact us to discuss your load, route and delivery requirements. Current fleet and compliance documentation is available on request.

Engineering

Cargo transportation, civil works, engineering fabrication and general supply for engineering-sector clients and their construction sites.

Food & beverage

Reliable, trackable cargo transportation that helps food and beverage clients keep production and distribution schedules on time.

Plastics & packaging

Cargo transportation and general supply support for plastics and packaging businesses moving materials and finished goods.

General logistics

End-to-end logistics support — paperwork, compliant insurance protocols, cargo transportation and real-time tracking — for logistics partners across the region.

Mining & Industrial Supply

Positioned for Zambia’s Copperbelt

Octane Transport is headquartered in Ndola, in the heart of Zambia’s Copperbelt mining region. Beyond cargo transportation, our General Supply service sources mining and industrial equipment, building & construction materials and PPE attire, backed by engineering fabrication — so mining, engineering and heavy-industry clients in and around the Copperbelt can arrange both the supply of goods and their delivery with one company.

Our client portfolio already includes logistics and petroleum-sector partners operating in this space. See our Mining & corporate procurement page for how we support procurement teams in this sector.
