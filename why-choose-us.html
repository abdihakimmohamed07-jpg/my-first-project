Why Choose Octane Transport

Our competitive strengths are grounded in how we actually operate — not generic claims.

Established Zambian Operator

A privately owned company headquartered in Ndola, in Zambia’s Copperbelt Province, with local knowledge of Zambian and regional routes.

Regional Reach

Cargo transportation throughout Zambia and the wider SADC region, backed by an established transport network.

Full-Service Capability

General supply, logistics, cargo transportation, real-time tracking and road transportation — under one accountable vendor.

Experienced, Qualified Team

A team of highly qualified staff with considerable experience in the transport and logistics industry.

Real-Time Cargo Visibility

Exact shipment location data and early delay awareness, so your operations team is never left guessing.

Compliance-Minded Logistics

Paperwork and compliant insurance protocols are managed proactively, ahead of every consignment.

Trusted Client Base

Working relationships with logistics and petroleum-sector clients including Reload Logistics, Impala Terminals, Hass Petroleum, Polytra Zambia, World Co. Logistics and Logistra Africa.

A Long-Term Partnership Approach

We pursue long-term relationship building, aiming for lasting, positive business relationships rather than one-off transactions.

What This Means For You

A Dependable, Accountable Partner

For procurement and supply-chain teams, that translates into fewer vendors to manage, clearer documentation, visible cargo status and a team invested in the relationship beyond a single shipment.
