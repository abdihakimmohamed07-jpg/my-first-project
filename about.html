About Octane Transport

A privately owned Zambian transportation, logistics and supply company, headquartered in Ndola and operating across Zambia and the SADC region.

Company Overview

Who Octane Transport Is

We are a privately owned company providing top-notch transportation, logistical and supply service solutions. Headquartered in Ndola, Zambia, we have substantial operating capacity, powered by a team of highly qualified staff with considerable experience in the industry.

We transport various equipment and supplies throughout the country and the SADC region. At Octane Transport, we work to ensure that your goods arrive safely and on time at their destination.

The Octane team pursues long-term relationship building, in the hope of forming lasting, positive business relationships with our clients.

Our Approach

Purpose, Direction & Values

Octane Transport has not yet published formal mission and vision statements; management is finalising these. In the meantime, the principles below reflect how we operate for our clients every day.

Our Purpose

To provide dependable transportation, logistics and supply solutions that get our clients’ goods to their destination safely and on time, throughout Zambia and the SADC region.

Our Direction

To keep building our operating capacity and regional reach, backed by a qualified, experienced team, so we can support a growing base of corporate, industrial and mining-sector clients.

Our Values

  • ✓Reliability & on-time delivery
  • ✓Compliance-minded operations
  • ✓Long-term client relationships
Our Commitment

Corporate Positioning

Octane Transport pursues long-term relationship building in the hope of forming lasting, positive business relationships with clients. That means clear communication, compliant paperwork and insurance protocols, real-time cargo visibility, and a team that treats every consignment as a priority — whether you are a wholesaler, a contractor, an industrial supplier or a mining-sector client.
