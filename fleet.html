Fleet & Equipment

Octane Transport maintains a well-serviced fleet of trucks to service our clients, supported by general supply and equipment-sourcing capability for construction, industrial and mining-related needs.

Procurement note: In line with our commitment to accuracy, this page describes our fleet and equipment capability by category rather than listing specific unit counts or models. A detailed fleet register — vehicle types, capacities and unit numbers — is available on request as part of vendor registration or tender due diligence. Request this information →

Cargo Trucks & Trailers

Our transport fleet underpins our Cargo Transportation and Road Transportation services, moving general cargo, produce and equipment throughout Zambia and the SADC region. The fleet is kept well-serviced to minimise breakdowns and protect delivery schedules.

  • ✓Configured for general and bulk cargo movement
  • ✓Used for produce, engineering and industrial consignments
  • ✓Maintained on a scheduled service programme

General Supply & Equipment Sourcing

Beyond our own transport fleet, our General Supply service sources and delivers building and construction materials, mining and industrial equipment, general office and hospital consumables, and PPE attire — backed by our own civil works and engineering fabrication capability.

  • ✓Building & construction materials
  • ✓Mining & industrial equipment
  • ✓PPE attire and general/hospital consumables

Tracking & Logistics Technology

Every consignment can be monitored through our real-time cargo tracking system, giving clients exact shipment location data and early awareness of delays — supported by our logistics team handling paperwork and compliant insurance protocols ahead of movement.

  • ✓Real-time shipment location visibility
  • ✓Early delay notification
  • ✓Documentation & insurance compliance support
