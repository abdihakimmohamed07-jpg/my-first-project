Who We Are

A Zambian Transport & Logistics Partner You Can Rely On

We are a privately owned company providing transportation, logistical and supply service solutions. Headquartered in Ndola, Zambia, we operate with substantial capacity, powered by a team of qualified staff with considerable experience in the industry.

We transport equipment and supplies throughout Zambia and the SADC region, and we work to ensure that our clients’ goods arrive safely and on time at their destination — while pursuing long-term, positive relationships with every client we serve.
